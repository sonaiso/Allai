# Formal Entities

- **ProofState**: immutable-stage logical carrier of closure facts and trace evidence.
- **Trace Event**: ordered, explainable event in constitutional progression.
- **Gate Law**: machine-checkable precondition/postcondition for each rank transition.
- **No-jump Invariant**: prohibition against skipping required closure ranks.
- **Ambiguity Outcome**: one of {resolved, suspended-with-reason}.
- **Replay Record**: deterministic reconstruction view of a trace chain.

## Foundational entities (v1)
- **Functional Reason Act**: transition chain from input presence to determination, understanding, linkage, judgement, and directed output.
- **Transition Validity Law**: constitutional method that decides whether movement between ranks/states is lawful.
- **Representation Law**: constitutional law governing meaning-to-form and form-to-meaning transition without invalid jump.
- **Execution Law**: constitutional law requiring conditions, contracts, and traceable effects for executable transitions.
- **Proof Necessity Constraint**: requirement that accepted transitions preserve consistency and admit necessity-style justification.
- **Thing/Method Separation**: each domain distinguishes substantive capability (thing) from validity law (method).
