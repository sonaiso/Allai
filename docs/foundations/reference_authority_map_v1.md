# Reference Authority Map v1

## Purpose
This document fixes the official pre-execution authority order for MVP v1 and defines which references are governing vs interpretive.

## Authority order (A -> E)

### A — Foundational authority (governing)
**Book: _Thinking (التفكير)_**

Primary scope:
- definition of reason (`'aql`)
- single rational method
- reality/sense/prior-information condition
- distinction between prior information vs prior opinions
- generalization of method across sensed objects, ideas, and speech understanding

Any new layer touching perception, differentiation, conception, initial judgement, or the bridge from reality to concept is measured first against A.

### B — Structural authority (governing)
**Book: _Islamic Personality, Part 1 (الشخصية الإسلامية ج1)_**

Primary scope:
- concepts as idea-meanings, not mere words
- concept formation by linking reality with information (and vice versa)
- mentality (`aqliyya`) as mode of perception
- disposition/nafsiyya as linkage of motives with concepts
- single rule (unified rule posture)
- holistic worldview basis (universe, human, life)

B provides the structural bridge between foundational cognition (A) and normative regulation (C).

### C — Normative/usuli authority (governing)
**Book: _Islamic Personality, Part 3 – Usul al-Fiqh (الشخصية الإسلامية ج3)_**

Primary scope:
- who judges / what governs
- judgement types
- reason vs revelation/shar'
- ruler and ruled-upon (hakim / mahkum 'alayh)
- cause, reason, condition, blocker (`'illa/sabab/shart/mani'`)
- indication directions (`jihāt al-dalāla`)
- certainty vs probability (qat'i / zanni)
- conflict handling and preference (ta'arud / tarjih)

Any layer touching judgement, indication, authority/proof, preference, or normative classification is measured first against C.

### D — High-layer explanatory authority (non-governing in executable gates)
**Qur'an**

Primary high-layer semantic scope includes names, exposition, reports, truth, certainty, remembrance, forgetfulness, heart (`qalb`), and inner-heart (`fu'ad`).

### E — Complementary interpretive authority (non-governing)
**Heidegger + Wittgenstein**

Used only as auxiliary interpretation for:
- disclosure before judgement
- rule/use/shared-practice framing

E does not govern constitutional gate law by itself.

## Governance policy
- Governing operational authorities for executable architecture and contracts are: **A, B, C**.
- D and E are interpretive/supportive references and cannot independently promote executable blockers.
- Any promotion from interpretive guidance to mandatory executable gate must be explicit in contracts/specs.

## Immediate execution implications (v1)
1. Start from a formal roadmap artifact because authority is now fixed.
2. Foundational gaps: **Docs + Contracts first**, then code.
3. Bounded operational gaps with stable surfaces: **Docs + Code together** after contract stabilization.
4. In "types of judgement / who judges", begin with neutral abstract structure:
   - perceptual judgement
   - indicational judgement
   - normative/legal judgement
   Then layer authority entities (individual, group, text, system).
5. Blocking policy in v1:
   - `concept_first`: critical new layers are blocking.
   - `legacy_text_first`: critical new layers remain trace-required and non-blocking.
