# Hierarchical Compositional Closure in Khabar and Insha Constitution v1

## Scope
This constitution defines minimum-first composition closure for **khabar** and **insha** within MVP v1.

## Governing doctrine (minimum-first)
Composition closure requires only what is necessary for legal transition.
Higher analytical categories are deferred unless promoted by explicit law.

## Three-circle policy
1. **Direct minimum conditions (mandatory gates)**
2. **Operational relational conditions (contextual, may become mandatory by law)**
3. **Deferred higher analysis (non-gating by default)**

## Minimum compositional ranks (7)
1. Compositional existence
2. Compositional designation (assignment/order)
3. Compositional possibility
4. Compositional identity type
5. Role distribution
6. Observable compositional effect
7. Final compositional closure

## Rank minima

### 1) Compositional existence
- Valid multi-part assemblage exists for composition.

### 2) Compositional designation
- Parts are bounded and ordered for operation.

### 3) Compositional possibility
- Assemblage admits legal relation and transition to identity typing.

### 4) Compositional identity type
- Composition is classified at minimum as khabar or insha eligible pattern.

### 5) Role distribution
- Minimum role structure exists (subject/predicate baseline for role distribution path).

### 6) Observable compositional effect
- Traceable compositional effect appears in structure/state.

### 7) Final compositional closure
- Mandatory prior compositional ranks are closed with no unresolved mandatory blockers.

## Transition constraints across pipeline
- No compositional entry without complete unified singular closure (`ready_for_composition=true`).
- No proposition closure without composition + communicative closure.
- No judgement without proposition closure + communicative closure + ambiguity outcome (resolved/suspended with reason) + valid trace chain.

## Khabar/Insha operating rule
At minimum closure level, khabar/insha distinction is identity-typing for transition legality.
Full rhetorical, logical, and semantic expansions are deferred to higher analysis layers.

## Non-mixing constraint
Do not mix in one gate:
- شرط with شارح
- رتبة with مقياس
- أصل with ملحق

## Constitutional trace requirement
Each rank must preserve trace evidence for:
- gate decision
- blockers
- transition readiness
- separation between minimum gate data and deferred analysis data
